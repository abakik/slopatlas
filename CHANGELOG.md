# Changelog

## Rename

Renamed from Sloplab to **Slop Atlas** (subtitle: *The default look of AI-generated interfaces, 2023–2027*). The in-page Atlas section is now **Atlas Grid**. New repository `abakik/slopatlas`; the old `abakik/sloplab` repository and Vercel project were deleted.

## v2.0 — evidence pass

- Re-checked every year against published sources (listed on the page under **Sources**). Each trait now carries an evidence tag: documented, reported, estimate or forecast.
- **2023** is now "AI hype, human-made": neon purple on midnight. This is how AI products presented themselves, since few site generators existed yet.
- **2024** is labelled as the Linear look, a style that predates AI and that generators reproduced. Interaction adds sparkles on every button (NN/g) and generative-UI tooling (Vercel AI SDK 3.0).
- **2025** is now "vibe-coded indigo": Inter, an indigo-to-purple gradient on white, three identical icon cards, metric tiles, accent-bar cards and minimal motion. This is the default that Anthropic (Nov 2025), Adam Wathan (Aug 2025), prg.sh and Impeccable describe. Ghibli imagery and the yellow cast stay in 2025.
- **2026** is now "post-purple editorial": cream, serif italics, terracotta, mono eyebrows, hairlines and numbered markers.
- The v1 2026 look (mono, dither, brutalist boxes) becomes the 2027 "Absorbed anti-slop" scenario. It is well supported as a human design trend, not as a generator default.
- Salience heatmap re-scored and extended to 24 tells, with sources in the tooltips.
- Recipe buttons have tooltips showing the trait, its tells and its evidence. They also work with keyboard focus.
