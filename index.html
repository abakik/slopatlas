Slop Atlas

A specimen bench for the default look of AI-generated interfaces, 2023 to 2026, with a forecast for 2027. Load a year to see its typical page. Swap single traits between years in the recipe. Every trait has a catalogue code (C·24 means colour, 2024) and an evidence tag: documented, reported or estimate.

      Method

      What counts as slop here. The look an AI tool produces when nobody specifies one: pages from prompt-to-app builders, images from default prompts, and the templates both learn from. Each year shows the cluster that was easiest to recognise at the time.

      Two kinds of year. 2023 shows how AI products presented themselves, mostly designed by people; few site generators existed yet. 2024 shows a look that predates AI, the Linear look, as generators reproduced it. From 2025 the looks are the generators’ own defaults, named as such by Anthropic, Tailwind’s creator and practitioners.

      Evidence tags. Documented: a cited source describes the trait for that period. Reported: commentary describes it, without a dated study. Estimate: the atlas’s own reading, with no source checked yet. Salience scores are estimates throughout, informed by the sources where they exist.

      How the forecast works. Four scenarios compete for each dimension. A trait's score is the scenario's driver value multiplied by a fixed affinity for that dimension. The weights are visible in the readout so the assumptions can be argued with.

      What it cannot predict. New model-specific artefacts. GPT-4o's yellow cast arrived without warning in 2025; the next one will too. Treat 2027 as a set of arguments, not a result.
