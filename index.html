    Method

    What counts as slop here. The look an AI tool produces when nobody specifies one: landing pages from prompt-to-app builders, images from default prompts, and the templates both learn from. Each year shows the cluster that was easiest to recognise at the time.

    Sources and confidence. The traits are a synthesis of design-community discussion, tool defaults (Tailwind, shadcn/ui, Geist, v0, Lovable, Bolt), image-model artefacts such as the 2025 yellow cast, and samples of generated pages. 2023 and 2024 are well documented. 2026 covers part of a year and is less certain.

    How the forecast works. Four scenarios compete for each dimension. A trait's score is the scenario's driver value multiplied by a fixed affinity for that dimension. The weights are visible in the readout so the assumptions can be argued with.

    What it cannot predict. New model-specific artefacts. GPT-4o's yellow cast arrived without warning in 2025; the next one will too. Treat 2027 as a set of arguments, not a result.
